# Authorship, study sources, and scope

Comedy Workbench was created for Max Chahuara with AI-assisted research, coding,
and editorial drafting. Its executable helper, instructions, data contract, and
teaching examples were written for this project. The method is an editorial
synthesis, not a claim to have invented the general principles of comedy writing.

The public project contains no vendored upstream implementation, model weights,
joke datasets, commercial skill bundle, book excerpts, or third-party scripts.
The private study archive contains downloaded snapshots for inspection; it is not
part of the project's MIT distribution. This was informed source study, not a
formal clean-room separation or a complete audit of every upstream line of code.

The [catalogue](research/sources.json) attributes each examined source, identifies
specific files or document sections, and records retained/rejected ideas. Source
availability and licenses were examined on the recorded retrieval date. GitHub
visibility alone does not establish permission to redistribute a repository.

Important distinctions found during study:

- LanguageTool's open core and its proprietary cloud AI rules are different.
- Story Architect has open-core and closed/commercial modules; Scrite's official
  applications and additional components have their own conditions.
- The RAGthoven framework is MIT; no explicit root license was found in its separate
  humor experiment repository. No experiment prompts or corpus are bundled here.
- Moltline's custom license restricts redistribution of its skill files and
  derivatives as competing packs. No such files or their question sequence are
  included; it was examined as a source with restricted reuse.
- screenwriting-skills distinguishes its own MIT material from quotations of books,
  scripts, and translations. Those quotations and corpus tables are not included.
- Witscript is a proprietary service. Only its public description was studied.
- OpenMic's paper-linked repository was unavailable; an alternative repository with
  that name was inspected separately and is not certified as the paper's artifact.
- Research paper access is not permission to relicense its text, images, or dataset.

Optional third-party tools remain separate programs under their own terms. They
are referenced for capabilities and limitations, not endorsed or redistributed.
No institution, researcher, software author, or comedian cited here endorses this
project. The MIT license applies to this project's original contributions.
